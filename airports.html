What's Included

  • All airports worldwide (large, medium, small, heliports, seaplane bases)
  • ICAO and IATA codes for commercial airports
  • Runway lengths in feet and meters
  • Runway widths and surface types (asphalt, concrete, grass, etc.)
  • Runway headings and designators (e.g., 09L/27R)
  • Geographic coordinates (latitude/longitude)
  • Airport elevation data
  • Country and municipality information

Database Schema

airports: id, ident (ICAO), iata_code, name, latitude, longitude, elevation_ft, type, iso_country, municipality runways: id, airport_id, length_ft, length_m, width_ft, surface, lighted, le_ident, he_ident, le_heading_deg, le_latitude, le_longitude

Example Queries

-- Find the longest runways SELECT r.length_ft, a.ident, a.name, a.iso_country FROM runways r JOIN airports a ON r.airport_id = a.id ORDER BY r.length_ft DESC LIMIT 10; -- Get all runways at JFK SELECT le_ident || '/' || he_ident AS runway, length_ft, surface FROM runways WHERE airport_ident = 'KJFK'; -- Airports with 4+ runways SELECT a.ident, a.name, COUNT(r.id) as runways FROM airports a JOIN runways r ON a.id = r.airport_id GROUP BY a.id HAVING runways >= 4 ORDER BY runways DESC;
