What's Included

  • All airports worldwide (large, medium, small, heliports, seaplane bases)
  • ICAO and IATA codes for commercial airports
  • Runway lengths in feet and meters
  • Runway widths and surface types (asphalt, concrete, grass, etc.)
  • Runway headings and designators (e.g., 09L/27R)
  • Geographic coordinates (latitude/longitude)
  • Airport elevation data
  • Country and municipality information

Historical Data Columns

icao - ICAO airport code (e.g., KJFK) iata - IATA airport code (e.g., JFK) airport_name - Full airport name country - ISO country code (e.g., US, GB, DE) latitude - Latitude in decimal degrees longitude - Longitude in decimal degrees runway - Runway designator (e.g., 09L/27R) year - Year of measurement (1985, 1990, 1995, ... 2024) length_m - Runway length in meters length_ft - Runway length in feet measurement_type - "modeled" (estimated from growth patterns) current_length_m - Current (2024) runway length for reference

Methodology

  • Historical lengths are modeled based on typical runway expansion patterns observed at major airports
  • Model assumes runways were ~85% of current length in 1985, with gradual expansion
  • Data points every 5 years: 1985, 1990, 1995, 2000, 2005, 2010, 2015, 2020, 2024
  • For actual satellite-measured data, contact us about Google Earth Engine analysis
